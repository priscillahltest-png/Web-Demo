Stock Room

The number updates as you enter a QTY: a Delivery Form subtracts it, a Return Form adds it. The stock numbers are locked (gray). Click Add Category & item or Remove Category & item and enter the password to unlock them and change the list; click either button again to lock them. These stock numbers are as of the day this pocket form was made and only move with your own entries. Add / Remove Category & item and the backup buttons all use the same backup password.

Project Names

Names for the signature lines

These names appear under INSPECTED BY, ITEMS WERE COLLECTED BY, DELIVERED BY and ACCEPTED BY on every phone and computer.
